# Transcription

> **Command:** `trec transcribe [id]`
> **Module:** Transcription · **Generated:** 2026-10-05 · **Updated:** 2026-10-23 (speech check)

## Overview
Converts a meeting's audio into timestamped text in the language the meeting was held in (Portuguese or English, detected per meeting), entirely on the Mac, once it has checked that someone speaks in it. This is the step that makes the meeting searchable and feeds the analysis.

## Fields and options
| Argument | Type | Required | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | text | No | all meetings in state `recorded` | Specific meeting to transcribe |

| `[transcription]` key | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `speech_check` | `true` | Before anything else, measure the speech in the whole recording with Silero VAD (the `vad_model`); one with under 2 s of speech is not transcribed (see Interactions). `false` transcribes every recording, for example to force one through |
| `language` | `"auto"` | `"auto"`: each meeting is transcribed in its detected language, and `transcript.json` gets `language_detection`. A code (`"pt"`, `"en"`): every meeting with `-l <code>`, no detection |
| `languages` | `["pt", "en"]` | The only languages detection may choose; the first is the fallback. Validated in both modes, used only when `language = "auto"` |
| `max_context` | `0` | whisper `-mc`: text of the previous window kept as context. `0` = none, so a hallucinated line cannot carry over; `-1` = whisper's default |
| `max_loop_share` | `0.10` | Above this share of segments in repetition loops the transcript is degraded. A line of at most 2 words counts as a loop only inside a run of 3 or more such lines |
| `retry_on_loop` | `true` | Transcribe a degraded transcript once more with the anti-loop settings |
| `retry_vad` | `true` | Use Silero VAD on that retry (needs `vad_model`, downloaded by the installer) |

## Interactions
- **Trigger:** manual command, or the automatic pipeline after `stop`.
- **Pre-processing:** the M4A is converted to 16 kHz mono WAV (the format whisper accepts) in a temporary folder.
- **Speech check (`speech_check = true`, the default):** first, under the meeting's lock and after the sample-rate check, `WhisperCppSpeechDetector` converts the audio the same way into its own temporary folder and runs `whisper-vad-speech-segments` (Silero VAD with the project's `-vt 0.5 -vsd 300 -vp 150`) over the whole recording; the audio length is read from the WAV. The pure rules in `domain/speech.py` turn the segments into a verdict: under 2.0 s of speech in total means **no speech**. Then whisper, the language detection, the retry and the speaker labelling do not run; the transcript is saved with no segments, the fallback language, no `health`, `language_detection` or `labels`, and `speech.found = false` (`transcript.txt` holds one newline); the meeting becomes `transcribed` and the pipeline goes on, and the analysis is the local empty one ("Empty transcript: no speech recognized.", no Claude call). A warning is logged (`no speech in <id>: 0.0 s of speech in 5.2 min of audio (under 2.0 s); whisper skipped, transcript empty`), and a recording of 60 s or more raises one "No speech in a recording" notification (no daily de-duplication; a notifier error is only logged and the transcript stays saved). With speech, transcription goes on as usual and the record gains `speech.found = true`. The check is advisory: with it off, or when it fails for any reason (tool or VAD model missing, timeout, output not in the expected format, an unreadable WAV), the meeting is transcribed exactly as before, with one warning (`speech check of <id> failed; transcribing without it: …`), no `speech` key, no `error.txt` and no notification. A wrong verdict is redone in three steps: `speech_check = false`, `trec transcribe <id>` and `trec analyze <id>` (the id form: `trec analyze` without an id picks only `transcribed` meetings), then `true` again; the daemon reads the config once, at start.
- **Voice activity detection (optional, off by default):** with `transcription.vad = true` and the Silero model present, whisper only transcribes segments containing speech. In a test with short sentences it made the text worse, so it stays off until it is measured on long meetings.
- **Language detection (`language = "auto"`, the default):** before transcribing, `WhisperCppLanguageDetector` takes 30-second samples spread evenly over the audio (one per 5 minutes of meeting, 3 to 12, never at the very first second), cuts each with ffmpeg into a temporary 16 kHz WAV and runs one `whisper-cli -l auto -dl` over all of them (the model loads once; about 2 s per sample on an M1, up to about 25 s per meeting). With the speech check, only the planned samples whose 30-second window holds at least 3.0 s of speech are cut and sent (the others are logged as `language samples of <id>: 5 of 8 without speech, not sent` and not listed in `language_detection.samples`), and a meeting with no recorded length is planned over the audio length the check measured instead of a single sample at its start; when no planned sample holds speech, the result is `no-usable-sample` and the fallback. A sample counts only when it heard an allowed language (`languages`) with probability 0.5 or more; the meeting's language is the one more than half of the counted samples heard, with at least 2 votes (1 only when the meeting was *planned* with a single sample, so one survivor of several planned samples gives `too-few-votes` and the fallback). Otherwise, or when detection fails for any reason, the first allowed language (`pt`) is used, with the reason recorded; a detection failure never fails the transcription and sends no notification. One language per meeting: a mixed meeting loses its minority language to translation. The rule is pure code in `domain/language.py`; the decision and its evidence are stored in `transcript.json` › `language_detection`.
- **Transcription:** model `large-v3-turbo-q5_0`, language = the detected one (or the fixed code), threads = half the cores by default, no text context between windows (`-mc 0`).
- **Health check:** after whisper, the transcript's health is computed: the share of segments inside repetition loops (the same text, ignoring case and punctuation, 5 or more times within 30 consecutive segments; a phrase written twice in one segment counts as the phrase; a line of at most 2 words counts only when the window rule flags it and it sits in a run of 3 or more consecutive segments that are all short and all flagged, whatever their texts, so "Okay." spread through a conversation is not a loop while "e aí" three times in a row is) and the minutes that have speech text outside the loops. Whisper's non-speech markers (`[BLANK_AUDIO]`, `(música)`, `♪`) are not text, and minutes after the last segment count as uncovered when the meeting's length is known. Above `max_loop_share` the transcript is **degraded**.
- **Retry:** a degraded transcript is transcribed once more with `-mc 0` and Silero VAD (about 30% slower), and the healthier result is kept: a pass that is not degraded (at most 10% of its segments in loops) wins when it keeps at least 85% of the other's minutes with text (a minute counts as covered by any line outside a loop, so a pass mostly in loops can look as if it covers more); below that, more minutes with text win, so a retry that loses more than 15% of the text, an empty or cut-short one included, never replaces the first pass; then fewer loops. The retry is skipped when it would only repeat the first pass (VAD already on, `retry_vad = false` with `max_context = 0`, or the VAD model missing), and when the Teams track was recorded at the wrong sample rate, since VAD cannot repair that audio; a retry that fails for any reason keeps the first pass.
- **Teams track at the wrong sample rate:** the counters `teams-tap` wrote for the Teams track (`tap.stats.json`) are checked too. When more than 5% of a track of 60 s or more was padded in short pads (under 0.5 s, too short to be logged as gaps), the track was recorded at a lower rate than it was written at, and the other participants' speech came out sped up and chopped. Healthy tracks pad under 3%; a 16 kHz headset written as 48 kHz padded 67%. The health is marked `rate_mismatch` with the short-padding share, a notification ("Teams audio recorded at the wrong rate") is sent, `minutes.md` opens with its own banner, and `trec status` / `trec transcribe` mark the meeting. It is independent of `degraded` and triggers no retry, since whisper cannot fix the audio. Meetings without counters (before 2026-10-14, Windows) are not checked; unreadable counters skip the check.
- **Still degraded:** the transcript is saved anyway with its health marked degraded, the analysis runs on it, a notification ("Degraded transcript") names the time ranges without text, and `minutes.md` opens with a warning banner listing them.
- **Output:** `transcript.json` (`speech` when the check ran: found, seconds of speech and of audio, and the number of speech segments; `language`, the language the transcript was made in, and `language_detection` when it was detected: language, reason, counted and winning votes, and each sample's offset, answer and probability; segments with start, end, text and, when labelled, `speaker`, plus `health`: loop share, minutes with text, uncovered minute ranges, degraded, retried, rate_mismatch, short_padding_share, and the `speakers` summary) and `transcript.txt` (one line per segment, `[hh:mm:ss] text`, or `[hh:mm:ss] Name: text` for a labelled one). Blank segments are discarded. Transcripts made before 2026-10-11 have no health.
- **Speaker labels:** last, inside the same lock and before saving, each speech segment is labelled from `speakers.jsonl` (the observer's events) and `energy.json` (the level of each raw track per 100 ms), both left by the recording; pure code, no model. A segment takes (1) the remote participant whose speaking turns (main and compact windows merged) cover at least 30% of it, the largest overlap winning; else (2) the user, when the microphone is at least 6 dB louder than the Teams track over it and above -50 dBFS; else no label. The user's label is `[user] name`, else the own tile's display name, else "Você". The thresholds (`LabelRules`) are constants, tuned by the PRD's gates. Missing events or energy give fewer labels, never an error; a failure computing them is logged and the transcript is saved unlabelled.
- **Labelling summary:** every transcript saved by this version carries a `speakers` summary: the roster (names seen), the user's label, lines labelled out of lines eligible, and, when nothing was labelled, the reason: `binary-missing`, `permission-missing`, `start-failed`, `observer-died`, `no-window` (the helper ran but read no Teams meeting window), `no-match` (tiles read, no line matched) or `no-observer` (no events file: feature off, Windows, or recorded before the feature). A transcript made before the feature has no summary.
- **Notification:** the first transcribed meeting of a calendar day that has no label for a reason the user can act on (`binary-missing`, `permission-missing`, `start-failed`, `observer-died`, `no-window`) raises one "Speaker labels unavailable" notification with the reason; later ones that day only log. `no-match` only logs; `no-observer` is silent.
- **Locking:** a `.lock` file in the folder prevents two simultaneous transcriptions of the same meeting.
- **No argument:** processes all pending meetings, reporting `ok (N segments, M min)` per meeting, a speech line (`Speech: 44.6 of 52.3 min (85%)`, or `Speech: none (0.0 s of speech in 5.2 min); whisper did not run` followed by a `WARNING:` line and no health, language or speakers lines, or `Speech: not checked (…)` when the check is off or failed), a health line (`Health: X% of segments in repetition loops, text in N of M min`, plus a warning with the ranges without text when degraded) and a language line (`Language: en (heard in 12 of 12 counted samples; 12 taken)`, or the fallback and its reason, or `Language: pt (fixed by [transcription] language)`) and a speakers line (`Speakers: 78% of 214 lines labelled (Felipe, Lucas …)`, or `Speakers: no labels (<reason>)`, printed after the health block, also for `no-observer`); failures become `error.txt` and the command exits with code 1 if any occurred. A degraded transcript is not a failure. At the end it suggests `trec analyze`.
- **Status and doctor:** `trec status` marks each meeting with a degraded transcript and counts them, marks a recording with no speech `· no speech` (no language marker) and counts those (`Recordings without speech: 2`), adds `· en` to a meeting whose transcript is not Portuguese, and adds `· speakers 78%` when labels exist or `· speakers 0% (<reason>)` for a zero-label reason (nothing for `no-observer` or a transcript without a summary); `trec doctor` shows `max_context`, the threshold and whether the retry is on (with VAD, without it, or off and why), a `speech check:` line right after it (on with the tool's path, `[WARNING]` when `whisper-vad-speech-segments` or the VAD model is missing, or off; it never fails the doctor), a `language:` line (detected per meeting among the allowed languages with the fallback, or one language for every meeting), and a `speaker labels` line (never failing the doctor): off, helper not built, Accessibility permission missing (with how to grant it), or ready with the outcome of the last recording; a `[WARNING]` when the permission is granted now but the last recording reported it missing.
- **Clear errors:** missing model (with the command to download it), missing audio, missing whisper-cli (with the Homebrew command), timeout (3 h).

## Integrations
| Integration | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ffmpeg | Conversion to 16 kHz mono (for whisper and for the speech check, one shared command); the 30-second clips of the language detection (`-ss` before `-i`) | — |
| whisper-vad-speech-segments (whisper.cpp 1.9.x, Homebrew `whisper-cpp`) | The speech check: Silero VAD over the whole recording | Prints `Detected <n> speech segments:` and `Speech segment <i>: start = <cs>, end = <cs>` (centiseconds); the parser requires the count to match the lines, so a format change is a failed check, never "no speech". Looked up like `whisper-cli` (PATH, then `<project>/tools`) |
| whisper-cli (whisper.cpp 1.9.x) | Speech recognition; language identification (`-l auto -dl -f <clip>…`, never `-np`) | `-oj` JSON; `-mc`; optional `--vad -vm` (always on the retry when the VAD model exists) |
| ggml models | whisper and VAD weights | `scripts/download-model.sh [name]` |

## Business rules
- Observed performance on a MacBook Air M1: 15 s of speech in ~30 s including model load; long meetings approach 1:4.
- The transcript remains even after the audio is deleted by retention.
- **Language detection evidence (2026-10-21, `docs/tasks/language-detection/adrs/adr-001.md`):** every Portuguese clip heard `pt` with p ≥ 0.99; the MARTA review heard `en` on all 12 clips; the vans meeting (English, loud and clipped audio) heard `en` on 3 clips and `is` (Icelandic) on 5, which is why only allowed languages count. Gate G1 on every stored meeting is recorded in `docs/REQUIREMENTS-AND-ARCHITECTURE.md` §10.
- Whisper hallucinations on silence ("Tchau, tchau", "Legenda por …") are handled by the speech check (a recording with no speech is not transcribed at all), by dropping short transcripts in the analysis and by the noise reduction in the mix.
- **Speech check evidence (2026-10-23, `docs/tasks/speech-check/`; gates G1 to G3 in `docs/REQUIREMENTS-AND-ARCHITECTURE.md` §10):** on the 29 stored recordings with audio, the two with no meeting in them (a 10.8-second near-silence and a 5.2-minute noise recording, both transcribed by whisper as invented Portuguese and labelled `en` by language detection) measured 0.0 s of speech, and every other recording 7.3 s or more (16% to 88% of its audio); the 2.0 s threshold sits between them. VAD took 0.2 to 25 s per recording (25 s for the 86-minute one, conversion included). The speech inside the language samples is what decides the samples that are sent: the vans meeting, whose silent windows heard `is` five times, now samples three windows with speech and gives `en` (3 of 3).
- **Short replies are not loops (2026-10-23):** the vans meeting's English retry (77 segments, 8 of them "Okay.", never more than 2 in a row) was 10.4% of segments in loops, so degraded, with no loop in it; under the short-line condition it is 0.0%. The MARTA meeting in English goes from 1.2% to 0.4%, the Portuguese meeting 2026-10-17_09-56-47 from 3.8% to 1.9% ("e aí" ×3 in a row), and the vans meeting forced to Portuguese stays degraded (68.7% to 65.7%). No stored transcript changes its `degraded` value.
- **Hallucination loops (2026-10-08 incident):** an 86-minute client meeting held largely in English, transcribed as `pt` with whisper's default text context, came out with "A CIDADE NO BRASIL" 1,761 times (70% of the lines) and no usable text in minutes 0–58; nothing warned the user. Re-transcribed with `-mc 0`: 4% repeated segments (natural "Ok.") and text in all 86 minutes; with VAD as well: 3% and 84 minutes, 30% slower. `-mc 0` left the word error rate of nine synthetic Portuguese meetings unchanged (2.9% → 2.8%). Hence `-mc 0` on every pass and VAD only on the retry. Evidence: `docs/tasks/prd-llm-wiki/evaluation.md` §14.1 and §14.7.
- **Why a window, not a global count:** counting a text's repeats over the whole meeting also counts natural fillers: "Entendeu?" and "Tá" made 9.8% of the segments of a healthy real 29-minute meeting (07/10), just under the 10% threshold. With the 30-segment window every healthy transcript measured 0% (13 real, 81 synthetic), the looped one 68% (text in 28 of 86 minutes), and its `-mc 0` re-transcription 1.0% (86 of 86). The window catches loops cycling through up to 7 lines; 30 is the widest that keeps the healthy meetings at 0%.
- **The 2026-10-08 meeting retranscribed by the product (2026-10-11):** the first pass with `-mc 0` came out healthy (text in 86 of 86 minutes, 12 min 47 s, no retry needed), and its new analysis covers the whole meeting instead of the last half hour.
- VAD stays off on the first pass: in a test with short sentences it cut off speech. Its effect on long meetings was measured only on the 2026-10-08 audio (above).
