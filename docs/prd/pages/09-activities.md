# Activities document

> **Commands:** `trec activities [--open]`, `trec activities curate <id>… | --since YYYY-MM-DD [--yes]`, `trec activities undo [--yes]` · automatic after each analyzed meeting, through the daemon's curator worker and the curation step of `trec analyze`
> **Module:** Activities · **Status:** delivered as a **shadow** (slice 1 of `docs/tasks/activities-curator/`): it runs beside the daily plan, which stays until the user decides the plan goes; the plan's removal is not documented here yet

## Overview
One Markdown document, `<data_dir>/activities/atividades.md`, lists everything still open for the user: their own actions, what they wait on from others, dates, and recent context, each line linked to the meeting it came from. A few minutes after a meeting is analyzed, a curator agent (one Claude call) updates it. An item leaves the document only by being archived with a reason, and only the user ticks or unticks one. The user opens the file in an editor, ticks what is done, writes their own lines, and the next pass reads those edits and respects them.

It exists because the plan has no section for an open action that is neither new nor overdue, shows an overdue action once, never accumulates other people's actions, ignores meetings analyzed after 6 pm, and only the model can close an action. Not every meeting activity is a task or a story, so the document also keeps decisions, questions, risks and ownerless items.

During the shadow the daily plan, `open_actions.json` and the story drafting after the plan are unchanged. The curator reads neither of them: its inputs are the document, the new analyses, transcript excerpts, a calendar and the user's edits.

## The document (`atividades.md`)
Content is Brazilian Portuguese, written by the model; markers and headings are the labels in `messages.ActivityDoc`.

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Title | `# Atividades` |
| Box | Between `<!-- curador:início … -->` and `<!-- curador:fim <stamp> -->`: `Atualizado <weekday dd/mm hh:mm> · após <meeting titles>` and `Foco`, 3 to 5 numbered lines naming an item in bold and why now. Rewritten on every pass; leave the two marker lines alone: the number in the closing one is the stamp of the pass that wrote the file, which tells the app after a crash whether a pass reached the file |
| `## Minhas ações` | The user's actions and deadlines, as checkboxes: `- [ ] **title** — p/ <for whom> · <date> · <priority> · <links>` |
| `## Aguardando outros` | Others' actions the user depends on, as checkboxes: `- [ ] **Name**: <what> · <date> · <context> · <links>` |
| `## Agenda e prazos` | Dated meetings and milestones not owned by the user, no checkbox |
| `## Contexto recente` | About ten lines: `Decisão:`, `Pergunta:`, `Risco:`, `Sem dono:` |
| Link block | Between `<!-- links:início … -->` and `<!-- links:fim -->`: one reference-style definition per meeting id used as a label, generated by the app (`[<meeting id>]: ../recordings/<id>/minutes.md`) |

Markers, only three: 💬 a suggestion about something a meeting said (who, meeting, `hh:mm:ss`, the literal quote, and "mark [x] if done"), ⚠️ not confirmed (`a confirmar: <reason>`), 💤 not mentioned for about three weeks (`sem menção desde <date> — ainda vale?`). Item lines carry absolute dates with the weekday; at most two sub-lines per item.

What the user may do in the file: tick, untick, delete lines, reword, change a date, add own lines, sub-lines and `##` sections, write `#história` on an item. All of it is the user's decision at the next pass. A line typed inside the box is moved to the right section; one the pass did not move, or typed while a pass was running, is put at the top of `## Minhas ações` by the app, unchanged.

### Rules the curator follows
- **Completion is the user's.** A meeting that says something is done, cancelled or reassigned adds a 💬 sub-line on the item; it never ticks, unticks or archives an open item.
- **The user's hand wins.** Lines the user wrote or changed are never reworded, moved, archived, marked 💤 or given links on their main line (a mention is a sub-line, `citado na <link>`). On lines the curator wrote, only date, owner or priority fields change, with a change note (`prazo mudou de <antes> para <depois> (<meeting dd/mm>, hh:mm:ss)`).
- **Progress scope.** A statement by X touches only items owned by X or waiting on X; `indefinido` gives a 💬 only; an intention is not progress.
- **A ticked item** gets ` ✔ <weekday dd/mm>`, sinks to the bottom of its section, and is archived (`concluído`) once ticked before the previous working day.
- **A deleted item** is archived as `removido por você` and not re-added unless a new meeting brings a new fact. If most of the last pass's additions vanish at once with no other nearby edit, the pass treats it as an editor overwrite, re-inserts them with a `voltou: parece que o editor sobrescreveu` suggestion and notifies.
- **Nothing is deleted.** What leaves goes to `arquivo/<YYYY-MM>.md` with date and a reason the model writes in Portuguese (typically `concluído`, `data passou (<date>)`, `respondida na …`, `resolvido na …`, `sem menção há 7 dias`, `duplicado de: <title>`, `removido por você`). Open items are never archived for age. Past agenda entries leave the next day; context entries 7 days after their last mention or when answered.
- **Unconfirmed items** (not confirmed by the verifier, or from a degraded transcript) carry ⚠️ and enter the focus only when overdue.
- **One code rule**, checked edit by edit on the body between the box and the link block: only an `archive` removes a checkbox line, and a `replace` must neither drop a checkbox line nor change how many are ticked. An edit that fails is sent back once with the reason; otherwise the pass fails and changes nothing. Lines no edit touches are kept byte for byte.
- **Rerun safety.** Items whose origin link is the meeting being curated are recognised as already present.
- A meeting with nothing extracted (no actions, statements, deadlines, decisions, questions, risks or next meetings) costs no call.

## Commands
| Command | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `trec activities` | Prints `Document: <path>`, `Updated <Mon 19/10 10:12> after <titles>`, the `Focus:` lines (the model's Portuguese, from the box), `Open: 7 yours · 3 waiting on others · 2 agenda · 5 context` (counts from the four headings; a heading the user renamed or removed is omitted), `Last pass: <when> — <summary>`, and `Not curated:` with one line per meeting analyzed in the last 10 working days that has no successful pass (`<id> (<title>): <reason> → trec activities curate <id>`; the reason is its `curation_error.txt` or `not attempted yet`). Weekdays are English (`Mon 19/10`). With no document: `No activities document yet. Build it from past meetings: trec activities curate --since YYYY-MM-DD`. Exit 0. It takes no lock and never waits for the curator. `--open` also opens the file with the default application (`open` on macOS, `os.startfile` on Windows, the latter never run on real Windows) |
| `trec activities curate <id>…` | Reruns the curator for analyzed meetings; reruns are safe. Prints `waiting for the curator…` first when a pass is running, then `Curating <title> (<dd/mm>)… ok (<n> edits) — <summary>`. A meeting that is not analyzed prints `Meeting <id> is <state>, not analyzed: run trec analyze <id> first.` (exit 1); an unknown id prints the usual `error: meeting <id> does not exist` |
| `trec activities curate --since YYYY-MM-DD [--yes]` | The bootstrap and a bulk rerun: every analyzed meeting held on or after the date, one pass per day with up to four meetings, oldest first. Asks first: `Curate 23 meetings in 9 passes using your Claude subscription (~25 min)? [y/N]:` (the wording follows `llm.provider`: `using the Anthropic API (spends API credit)` with `api`; the estimate is passes × 160 s rounded up to 5 minutes). `n` prints `Nothing curated.` (exit 0). `--yes` skips only the question. Giving ids and `--since` together, or neither, is an error. A failed pass of several meetings is re-run one meeting at a time; a meeting that fails alone prints `failed: <reason>`, gets `curation_error.txt`, and the command exits 1 at the end after trying the rest |
| `trec activities undo [--yes]` | Restores the document to before the last pass that changed it: prints the pass (`Last pass: <when> after <titles> (<summary>)`) and a diff (`--- now` / `+++ before the pass`), says how many lines edited after the pass will be discarded, and asks `Restore the document to before this pass? [y/N]:`. The preview is always printed; `--yes` skips only the question. After the undo the next pass sees again every edit made before and during the undone pass, and the command names the meetings to rerun (`Restored. Run trec activities curate <ids> to curate that meeting again.`). Messages, all exit 1: `The curator is working; try again in a few minutes.`, `No pass to undo.`, `A new pass ran while you were deciding; run trec activities undo again.` (document untouched). When the archive changed after the pass, its entries are left in place and the command says so. Failed, skipped and undo records are never the target; undoing the first (priming) pass is allowed. No LLM prerequisite is needed |
| `trec analyze [id]` | After its loop, curates the meetings it analyzed successfully (four per pass, oldest first) with the same output lines; skipped silently when `[activities] enabled = false` (see the [analysis page](./03-analysis.md)) |
| `trec status` | The line `Activities: 7 yours · 3 waiting on others (last pass Mon 19/10 10:12)` replaces `Open actions` (`not created yet (trec activities curate --since YYYY-MM-DD)` without a document; `unreadable (...)` and exit 1 when the pass log or the document cannot be read) |
| `trec doctor` | `[ok ] Activities document: <path> (ok, last pass <when>)`, `[WARNING] … (N meetings not curated (trec activities))`, or `[info] … (not created yet (…))`. Information only: it never changes the exit code |

Notifications (macOS Notification Center, Windows toast): `Activities updated`, whose body is the curator's one-line Portuguese summary, only when the pass added an action of the user's, a 💬, changed a date of the user's, removed an item other than by a tick, or suspected an editor overwrite (and `[activities] notify = true`); `Activity curation failed`, always on failure: `<title> (<id>): <error>. It will be retried; or run trec activities curate <id>.`

## Configuration
| Key | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `[activities] enabled` | `true` | `false` turns off only the automatic triggers (the daemon's worker and the curation step of `trec analyze`); the `trec activities` commands still run |
| `[activities] model` | the `[llm]` model | Curator model with `provider = "api"` |
| `[activities] cli_model` | `"opus"` | Curator model alias with `provider = "claude-code"` |
| `[activities] effort` | `"medium"` | Reasoning effort (`low` … `max`) |
| `[activities] notify` | `true` | Whether a pass may send `Activities updated` |
| `[activities] keep_versions` | `50` | Passes whose version files are kept for undo (at least 1) |

The curator uses its own transport on the `[llm]` provider and logs to `llm_usage.jsonl` (`curate:<id>`, `curate-repair:<id>`). `[user] name` is sent to it. The Windows installer takes `-NoActivities` (`[activities] enabled = false`); `scripts/config_tool.py` reads and writes the key.

## Interactions
- **Trigger.** `Pipeline` calls an `after_analysis` hook once when a meeting reaches `analyzed` in that run; the daemon's `CuratorWorker` thread queues it. A round takes the woken meeting, up to three more, and every meeting analyzed in the last 2 working days (today is day 0; Monday to Friday, holidays ignored) with no successful pass and fewer than 2 failed attempts curated alone. It runs them in passes of four, oldest first, under the lock. The daemon queues those pending meetings when it starts, so a pass lost to a restart is rebuilt. A pass never blocks recording, transcription or analysis, and a hook failure is only logged.
- **One pass** (`CurateMeetings`): under the OS lock, reads the document, calls the curator, applies its edits to the file as it is when the answer arrives (so an edit made while the model was thinking survives), repairs failed edits once, then commits in a recoverable order: version files, archive entries, the document, `base.md`, and as the commit point the line in `log/activities.jsonl`. After a hard stop the next lock entry rolls the pass forward or back by comparing the box stamp.
- **Failure.** Refusal, truncation, schema violation, timeout, auth, edits that cannot be applied after the repair, or a checkbox rule violation fail the pass: the document is untouched, `recordings/<id>/curation_error.txt` holds `Activity curation failed: <reason>` (cleared by a later success), a notification is sent, and the daemon retries (at most 2 failed attempts alone). Curation errors never make the meeting `failed` and never write `error.txt`.
- **Concurrency.** Passes, undo and the worker share one OS lock; the user's editor is not locked, which is why edits made during a pass are read at the next one. `undo` never waits: it reports the curator as busy.
- **Restart.** The worker is Python code in the daemon: it starts after `trec agent restart`. The CLI commands run the curator in their own process and need no restart.

## Integrations
Claude through the existing structured transport (API or headless Claude Code, 900 s timeout); macOS `open` / Windows `os.startfile` for `--open`. No new external service.

## Relationships
- **From:** [Analysis](./03-analysis.md) (state `analyzed`; the progress statements and `for_whom` it extracts, and the minutes each line links to). **To:** the user's editor. `trec stories` still reads the plan during the shadow; reading tagged `#história` items from the document is a later slice.
- [Daily plan](./06-daily-plan.md) runs beside it until the user decides it goes.
- Files and the pass log: [data layout](../appendix/data-layout.md).

## Business rules
- Only the transcript text, excerpts of it and the analyses leave the Mac, as for the analysis.
- Rollout: the curator runs in the shadow of the planner for about a week before the user decides; the quality checks before it (progress-statement precision and a synthetic sequence of days) are task 8 of the plan and run with the user's approval.
