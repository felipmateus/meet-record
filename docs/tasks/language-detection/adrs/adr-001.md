# ADR-001: Detect from samples spread over the meeting, counting only allowed languages

## Status

Proposed

## Date

2026-10-21

## Context

The transcriber is pinned to `-l pt`. Two of 27 recorded meetings were held in English and came out as Portuguese text that was never said (see `_spec.md` › Evidence). whisper.cpp offers language identification in two forms: `-l auto` on the transcription itself, and `-dl` (identify and exit). Both decide from one 30-second window: the start of the audio passed to them.

Measured on real meetings (whisper.cpp 1.9.4, `large-v3-turbo-q5_0`, M1, 2026-10-21):

- Portuguese meetings: every clip `pt`, p ≥ 0.99 (15 clips, two meetings).
- MARTA review (English throughout): every clip `en` (12 clips, p 0.65–0.9998).
- Vans meeting (English, loud and clipped audio): `en` on 3 clips, and `is` (Icelandic) on 5 clips with p 0.90–0.98. The `is` clips are English speech.

User decisions of 2026-10-21: one language per meeting (mixed meetings are rare); allowed languages `pt` and `en`; a spec before the code.

## Decision

Before transcribing, take 30-second samples spread evenly over the meeting: one per 5 minutes, 3 to 12, never at the very first second. Run them through one `whisper-cli -l auto -dl` call with one `-f` per sample (the model loads once; the encoder, about 2 s per sample on the M1, is the main cost). Count a sample only when its answer is an allowed language with p ≥ 0.5. The meeting's language is the one more than half of the counted samples heard, with at least 2 votes (1 when the meeting has only one sample). Otherwise, or when detection fails, the fallback is the first allowed language (`pt`). The whole meeting is then transcribed in that language with today's settings.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: `-l auto` on the transcription

- **Description**: pass `-l auto` to the existing transcription call; whisper picks the language from the first 30 seconds and uses it for the whole file.
- **Pros**: one flag; no extra process; no new port.
- **Cons**: one window decides, and it is the meeting's start, which is often greetings, waiting or silence. Measured on 2026-10-22: whisper answers `en` on silence (p 0.38) and on noise (p 0.48–0.51), and a 60-second file of English then Portuguese got one `en` answer, after which the Portuguese half came out as "What? What? What?". A Portuguese meeting that opens in silence would lose its speech. Nothing stops a non-allowed answer: on the vans meeting it would be one clip away from transcribing an English meeting as Icelandic. There is no record of why.
- **Why rejected**: a single unconstrained guess is exactly the failure the measurements show.

### Alternative 2: detect and transcribe per stretch

- **Description**: split the meeting into stretches (fixed blocks or VAD segments), detect each stretch's language and transcribe each stretch in its own language, then stitch the timestamps.
- **Pros**: handles meetings that switch language.
- **Cons**: cuts words at block edges; a whisper call per block (the model reloads, or a server mode is needed); timestamp offsets threaded through the health check, the retry and speaker labelling; much more to test.
- **Why rejected**: user decision of 2026-10-21 (mixed meetings are rare). The decision keeps it possible later: the sample record already shows a split meeting (`counted` against `votes`), which is the signal a follow-up would act on.

### Alternative 3: ask the LLM after a first transcription

- **Description**: transcribe in Portuguese, ask Claude whether the text is a translation of English speech, and re-transcribe.
- **Pros**: no new whisper usage.
- **Cons**: transcribes long meetings twice; spends the subscription per meeting; the forced-Portuguese text is fluent nonsense that hides the original language; detection would leave the Mac's local-only step.
- **Why rejected**: slower, costlier and less reliable than whisper's own identification on the audio.

### Alternative 4: samples restricted to speech (VAD or `energy.json`)

- **Description**: choose sample windows where speech was detected (Silero VAD, or the per-track energy that recordings since speaker attribution keep).
- **Pros**: fewer wasted samples on silence.
- **Cons**: VAD is off by default and its model is optional; `energy.json` is missing on older meetings and on any meeting whose energy pass failed; the allow-list and the probability floor already discard what silence and noise produce.
- **Why rejected**: not needed for the measured cases; kept as a possible improvement if gate G1 shows wasted samples.

## Consequences

### Positive

- English meetings are transcribed in English with no setting per meeting.
- Portuguese meetings run the exact transcription command they run today.
- Every decision is explainable from `transcript.json` (samples, answers, probabilities, reason).

### Negative

- About 25 seconds more per meeting on the dev Mac at the 12-sample cap: about 0.6 s of model load plus about 2 s of encoder per sample. CPU-only Windows is unmeasured.
- A mixed meeting loses its minority language to translation.
- A new dependency on whisper-cli's log line format.

### Risks

- A false switch of a Portuguese meeting to English. Mitigated by the majority-and-two-votes rule, gate G1 on all 25 Portuguese meetings, and a one-line fix (`language = "pt"`).
- A whisper.cpp upgrade changes the answer line. Detection then falls back to `pt` with a warning; the slow real-whisper test catches it.
- Bad audio stays bad. On the vans meeting, the English first pass still loops (69%); only the anti-loop retry, now run in English, clears it (see References). Detection fixes the language; the retry is still what repairs a loop.

## Implementation Notes

- Do not pass `-np` to the detection call: it suppresses the `auto-detected language` line.
- Map answers to files by the `processing '<path>' (` line that precedes each answer, not by position, so a clip whisper skips cannot shift the answers. Match the clip's basename (`c<i>.wav`, chosen by the adapter), not the full path: a temporary directory can contain an apostrophe.
- Whisper exiting 0 without a single answer line is a detection failure (`Err.WHISPER_NO_LANGUAGE`), not a meeting without usable samples: after a whisper.cpp upgrade, the operator must be sent to the log.
- The thresholds are `domain/language.LanguageRules` constants, not configuration (like `LabelRules`).
