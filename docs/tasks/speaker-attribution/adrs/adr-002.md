# ADR-002: The user's own lines from the two tracks' energy

## Status

Proposed

## Date

2026-10-16

## Context

The user's own tile ("Vídeo de mim mesmo, …") exposes name and mute state but no frame element, so the Accessibility signal of ADR-001 never says when the user speaks. The recorder already writes the microphone and the Teams output as two tracks aligned to the same start instant, and the Teams track never carries the user's own voice (Teams does not loop the microphone back). Remote speech that leaks from the Mac's speakers into the microphone is present on both tracks, louder on the Teams track.

## Decision

Measure each raw track's RMS level per 100 ms before the tracks are deleted (one ffmpeg pass per track, saved as `energy.json`) and attribute to the user every line that no remote turn covers at least 30% of, when the microphone's mean level over the line is at least 6 dB above the Teams track's and above −50 dBFS. The user's label is the configured name, else the own tile's display name, else "Você".

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: By elimination

- **Description**: any line without a remote speaker is the user's.
- **Pros**: no measurement.
- **Cons**: silence, hallucinated lines and stretches with a covered window would all become the user's.
- **Why rejected**: wrong labels where the pipeline knows nothing.

### Alternative 2: Transcribe the microphone track separately

- **Description**: run whisper on `mic.wav` and `tap.wav` apart and merge by time.
- **Pros**: perfect separation of the user's words.
- **Cons**: doubles transcription time, merging two timelines of segments is error-prone, the mix with noise reduction is what gives today's accuracy.
- **Why rejected**: cost and complexity against a cheap energy comparison.

### Alternative 3: Teams' own mute state

- **Description**: the own tile's "Mudo ativado/desativado" text tells when the user could speak.
- **Pros**: text, already read.
- **Cons**: unmuted is not speaking; the user may talk muted in the room.
- **Why rejected**: not a speaking signal; kept as diagnostics only.

## Consequences

### Positive

- No model; two numbers per 100 ms; robust to leakage because of the margin.
- Works even when the Teams window is covered.

### Negative

- Two extra ffmpeg passes before mixing (seconds for an hour of audio).
- A hallucinated whisper line during the user's breathing or room noise above −50 dBFS could be labelled as the user's; the hallucination-loop guard still applies.

### Risks

- Thresholds tuned on one Mac and headset: gate G1 checks them; they are constants in one place.

## Implementation Notes

`energy.json` is written atomically or not at all; a measurement failure logs and leaves the user's lines unlabelled. The arrays are as long as the longer track.
